🎁 Promotion Value — Boost vs Early-Win Token

A 50% profit boost pays 1.5 × EVraw + 0.5 × (1 − p) — so at a fair price it returns +25% on an even-money bet and +40% on a +400 one. An early-win token instead adds a fixed 10.3 points of win probability, paid at those same odds. Both therefore prefer longer prices, and which one wins depends on the price rather than on the promotion. A boost is all-purpose and a token is not — the token can only settle a moneyline early, which is why most rows here have no token figure at all and why the boost's real advantage is the selections it can reach.
