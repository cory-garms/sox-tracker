📊 Market Consensus — Draftkings vs the Field

No positive-EV price on the board. Every selection here is priced at or behind the consensus of the other books — the closest is Patrick Sandoval over 5.5 at +105, -0.49%. That is the normal state of a liquid market and is what the vig looks like from the inside.

Under a 50% profit boost the same board reads differently: Boston Red Sox moneyline at +137 becomes +27.48%. A boost multiplies profit rather than stake, so its value climbs as the price lengthens — which is why the boosted column does not rank the same way as the raw one. That column is conditional and most of it is not available. It applies only to whoever holds the token, only to one bet, and the numbers in it sit beside raw prices that are negative — a boost worth more than the house edge does not make the underlying bet good, it makes the promotion good. Read the EV column for the bet and the boosted one only if you are actually holding a boost.
