Put a number on it: if he were a league-average power hitter — about 3.5% of at-bats ending in a home run — the chance of 9 or more in 65 is 0.0004, roughly one in 2,340. That is small enough to say the rate has genuinely changed and far too small a sample to say what it changed to.
